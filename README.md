# Theatre Animator

Historically careful animations of ancient Greek theatre and thought, rendered as realistic 3D video. Every caption says how sure we are of what is on screen: attested by ancient texts, inferred by scholars, or reconstructed by us. See `shared/STYLE.md`.

## Animations

| Slug | Subject | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `animations/ekkyklema` | The ekkyklema, the platform rolled out to reveal interior scenes | Research and storyboard approved; 3D look test in progress |
| `animations/ajax-ekkyklema` | The ekkyklema in Sophocles' *Ajax* (346–595) | Planned |

## Each animation folder
- `NOTES.md`: research questions and the primary passages checked
- `SOURCES.md`: ancient and modern sources, with findings tagged [A] attested, [I] inferred or [R] reconstruction
- `STORYBOARD.md`: beats, captions, and the approved reconstruction choices
- `layout.svg`: true-scale layout drawing
- `3d/`: the Blender scene script, the shot list, asset licences and look-test renders
- the finished video

`animations/ekkyklema/index.html` is an abandoned 2D diagram draft, kept for reference only.
